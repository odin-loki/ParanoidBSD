hbsd/src/lib/libc/locale/mbrtowc.c - body is nothing but FIX_LOCALE()/XLOCALE_CTYPE(locale)->__mbrtowc dispatch through the HardenedBSD-private xlocale ctype table declared in mblocal.h/xlocale_private.h, which is not part of this batch; any substitute conversion backend would change behaviour, so no faithful port is possible.
hbsd/src/lib/libc/locale/c32rtomb.c - after the D90 range check the body tail-calls wcrtomb_l() and takes its mbstate_t from XLOCALE_CTYPE(locale)->c32rtomb; both are HardenedBSD-private xlocale internals absent from this batch, and the per-locale state cannot be reproduced faithfully.
hbsd/src/lib/libc/locale/mbstowcs.c - body consists of FIX_LOCALE() plus a call to XLOCALE_CTYPE(locale)->__mbsnrtowcs with SIZE_T_MAX; the per-locale __mbsnrtowcs backend and SIZE_T_MAX come from HardenedBSD-private headers not in this batch, so it cannot be ported faithfully.
